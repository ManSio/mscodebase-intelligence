## 🪟 Windows Specifics: ZED_WORKTREE_ROOT

**Status:** ⚠️ Always `<unset>` on Windows (Zed bug #36019)

The `ZED_WORKTREE_ROOT` environment variable is NOT set on Windows.
This is a known Zed bug, closed without a fix.
